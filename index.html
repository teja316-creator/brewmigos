The Brewmigos story

Two recipes. One counter.

Behind every Brewmigos order, two things are being made side by side: a cookie getting mixed, loaded and baked, and a coffee getting ground, bloomed and poured. Scroll to watch both — they meet at the end.

The cookie Step 1 of 3 · The dough

It starts with butter, sugar and far too many chips.

Every cookie begins in a home kitchen bowl. The dough is thick and heavy on purpose, then stuffed edge to edge with chips, chunks and Biscoff. We don't measure the good stuff with a teaspoon.

  • Dark, milk & white chips
  • Biscoff spread
  • Macadamia
  • KitKat & M&Ms
The coffee Step 1 of 3 · The grind

Meanwhile, a bean becomes coffee.

While the dough rests, the coffee starts as whole beans. We grind them just before they're brewed, so the smell hits you before the cup does.

The cookie Step 2 of 3 · The bake

Pulled warm. Never overbaked.

Scooped into fat balls and into the oven they go. Crispy at the edge, gooey in the middle — we pull them a minute early on purpose, because that's the whole point.

The coffee Step 2 of 3 · Bloom & pour

A slow pour, while the cookies rise.

Hot water hits the grounds and they bloom — puffing up and letting go of their gas. Then a slow, steady pour, round and round, until the carafe fills. No pods, no shortcuts.

The cookie Step 3 of 3 · Cool & box

A short rest, then into the box.

Just long enough on the rack to hold their shape — not long enough to lose the goo. Then they're boxed, tied up and packed for the pop-up.

The coffee Step 3 of 3 · Into the cup

Hot, cold or green — your call.

A short espresso, a creamy oat latte, a cold brew steeped for a full day, or a ceremonial-grade matcha. Every one made at the stall, right in front of you.

The end of both stories

A warm cookie in one hand. A fresh coffee in the other.

That's where the two stories meet — at our pop-ups around Hyderabad, served by the four of us.
